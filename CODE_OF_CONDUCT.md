# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AI Watchtower a harassment-free experience for everyone regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our standards

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful. Disagreement is fine, personal attacks are not.
- Assume good faith. Ask before assuming malice.
- Keep issues on-topic. This is a governance tool for Azure AI Foundry, not a general Azure or AI forum.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks in any form
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Off-topic advocacy, marketing, or recruitment

## Enforcement

Reports of abuse should go to the maintainers via email (address in the repo's About section) or a private GitHub security advisory. Maintainers will review and respond within 7 days. Enforcement actions may include warnings, temporary bans, or permanent bans from the project.

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, PRs, discussions, and any project-affiliated communication channels.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
